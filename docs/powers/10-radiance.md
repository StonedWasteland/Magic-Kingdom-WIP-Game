# Power 10 — Radiance

**Identity:** *Light that mends and guards. Where the regenerator heals what's already broken, you
make sure it never breaks — shields of hardlight, a cleansing glow that burns away affliction, and
a radiance that strips the dark of everywhere it hides.*

- **Domain:** Elemental (light)
- **Specificity:** Broad support — guard, cleanse, reveal, and a little holy fire
- **Material:** None — generative light
- **Role:** Support / guardian
- **Intrinsic tags:** `Elemental` `Light` `Generative` `Sustained` `No-Material`

> **Two engine concepts this power forces.**
> **(1) Shields (`Barrier`) — preventive HP.** A heal restores HP *after* the hit; armor reduces
> *each* hit; a `Barrier` is a **separate pool that soaks incoming damage before HP is touched**, and
> drains as it does. The engine routes damage through `Barrier` first, then the body. A third,
> distinct defensive layer — and the complement to Wren: she heals what broke, Radiance keeps it from
> breaking.
> **(2) Unmake — the dispel verb, weaponized.** Everything so far *applies* tags. **`Unmake`**
> **removes** them — and not surgically: it strips **every** tag off a target, *buffs and debuffs
> alike*, then **heals 2 HP per tag unmade** (light feeding on the magic it unweaves). On a
> debuff-drowned ally it's a big cleanse-and-heal; the honest cost is it also wipes that ally's own
> `Barrier`/stance/well — so it's a reset, not a scalpel. (Designed against buffed enemies too — it'll
> feast hardest once Pack Alpha and other buff-stacking foes exist; it mirrors the Disruptor exactly.)
> (Note: unlike Wren's regen, Radiance is *light*, not flesh — fire doesn't `Cauterize` it. The
> fire-proof support.)

## Expressions

### ◈ Support — Aegis & Unmake *(starter)*
Two faces of the guardian's light:
- **Aegis** — project a `Barrier` onto an ally: an absorption pool that eats incoming damage before
  their HP.
- **Unmake** — strip **all** tags off a target (buffs *and* debuffs) and **heal 2 per tag removed.**
  A cleanse-and-heal for a debuff-laden ally (you also wipe their own buffs — a reset, not a scalpel);
  a buff-feast against enemies. The mirror of the Disruptor.
- **Cost:** medium Strain each.

### ◈ Defense — Sanctuary
Raise a radiant aura on your band: allies inside take reduced damage, and anyone Engaged with you
eats `Light` retaliation.
- **Cost:** medium Strain, sustained.
- **Unlock (natural):** shelter allies in the light several times.

### ◈ Utility — Beacon
Burn back the dark: `Reveal` hidden/stealthed foes, dispel `Blinded`/darkness/concealment, and
solve unlit obstacle tags. The hard light that leaves nowhere to hide.
- **Cost:** trivial Strain.

### ◈ Offense — Radiant Lance
A lance of searing light, Near/Far: `Light` damage that ignores part of a target's defense (holy
glare) and can `Blind`. Extra bite versus shadow/`Umbra`-type foes.
- **Cost:** low Strain.

### ◈ Signature — Dayspring *(capstone)*
Dawn breaks at will: **mass `Barrier`** on the whole squad, **mass `Cleanse`** (strip every debuff
off your allies at once), and a `Light` flare that `Blinds` every enemy — devastating to anything
that lives in the dark.
- **Cost:** high Strain. **Backlash:** the light gutters out — no `Barrier`/`Cleanse` possible for
  several rounds (the squad fights raw while you recover).
- **Unlock:** story milestone + "shielded the squad through a near-wipe."

## Material rules
None — generative light, never runs dry. **The trade is denial, inverted:** Radiance is *strongest*
in the open and *weakest* where the light is eaten — `Umbra`/shadow zones and anti-light foes blunt
it. **Resolve** caps the sustain.

## Combos
- **Sets up:** `Barrier` (prevents damage), `Cleanse` (removes affliction), `Blinded`, `Reveal`.
- **Pays off (the squad's lifeline):** **`Barrier` the gravity tank** — Gravity Anchor pulls the
  *whole army's* aggro onto itself, and a pre-emptive shield keeps it standing where reactive healing
  might arrive too late. `Radiant Lance` on a `Shred`ded, `Entangled` cluster lands amplified and
  can't be dodged. And `Cleanse` un-does enemy `Shred`/`Slow`/`Burning` on your own squad.
- **Cancelled by:** `Umbra`/shadow (anti-light), burst big enough to pop the `Barrier` in one blow,
  Light-immune foes.
- **Tension:** `Beacon`'s light **dispels concealment** — including your *own* `Screen`/smoke. Reveal
  the enemy and you may un-hide your squad too. Coordinate the light with the dark.

## Environment
- **Loves:** `Open Sky` · `Enclosed` *lit* · anywhere the squad is taking focused fire
- **Hates:** `Umbra`/shadow zones · deep dark · Light-immune enemies

## Strain profile
Sustained and steady, like a second tank's upkeep — but spent on *prevention*. The skill is timing
the `Barrier`: shield the blow *before* it lands, not the wound after. Pairs with Wren-style reactive
healing as a two-layer sustain, but stands alone fine as the fire-proof guardian.
