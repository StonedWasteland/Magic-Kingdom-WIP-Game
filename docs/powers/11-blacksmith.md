# Power 11 — Blacksmith

**Identity:** *Forge weapons from nothing, each a different answer. A flameblade for the frozen, a
maul for the armored, a chain for the runner. The armory is the power — summon the tool the moment
demands, and when it shatters, forge the next.*

- **Domain:** Conceptual (manifestation / conjuration)
- **Specificity:** Broad arsenal, each weapon narrow — a master of *fit*, not of force
- **Material:** None carried — manifests weapons from will (each costs to summon / shatters in time)
- **Role:** Striker / flex-martial — fills whatever gap the fight has
- **Intrinsic tags:** `Conceptual` `Conjure` `Versatile` `Melee` `No-Material`

> **The engine concept this power forces — the ARSENAL (multi-mode loadout).** Metal Skin gave us
> *one* stance; Blacksmith gives a **menu** of them. Summoning a weapon equips a `Wielding:X` mode
> tag that grants *that weapon's* package of tags — `Wielding:Flameblade` carries `Fire`/`Burning`,
> `Wielding:Maul` carries `Crushing`/`Knockback`, `Wielding:Chain` carries `Entangled`. Swap freely
> (an action, a little Strain) to swap your whole kit. It's the **deliberate** cousin of Probability
> Pull's `Drawn-Kit` — you don't *roll* your tools, you **forge the one you need** — and it's the
> capstone of the engine thesis: a power whose gimmick is **assembling other powers' tags on demand.**
> The catch is balance, not mechanics: each conjured tag is *weaker* than the specialist's (a
> flameblade burns less than Ember). Versatility, never mastery.

## Expressions

### ◈ Offense — Summon Arms *(starter)*
Conjure a weapon and strike; the weapon decides the tags:
- **Flameblade** → `Fire` + `Burning` · **Maul** → `Crushing` + `Knockback` · **Glaive** →
  `Piercing` + reach (hits Near) · **Chainwhip** → `Slashing` + `Entangled`.
Pick the blade that fits the foe — and the field (a flameblade to ignite, a maul to knock into a hazard).
- **Cost:** low Strain to summon-and-swing; a swap mid-turn costs the action.

### ◈ Defense — Bulwark Arms
Forge a guard — tower shield or parrying blade: `Barrier`/`Cover` and a riposte (`Slashing` to
whoever strikes you). Become your own front line.
- **Cost:** medium Strain, sustained while held.

### ◈ Utility — Forge Tools
Conjure the *right tool*, not a weapon: a key, a pry-bar, a grapple, a torch. Improvise past
`Barred`/`Locked`/`Sealed`/`Chasm` obstacles — the smith always has the implement.
- **Cost:** trivial Strain.

### ◈ Support — Arm the Squad
Forge a blade *for an ally*: hand them a temporary weapon, or temper their strikes with a tag
(their next hits carry `Fire`, or `Bleeding`, or `Armor-Pierce`). Make the medic a fighter, or the
artillerist a brawler.
- **Cost:** medium Strain.
- **Unlock:** arm an ally a few times (natural).

### ◈ Signature — Panoply *(capstone)*
Unleash the whole armory: a storm of floating conjured blades that strike **every** enemy in a band
(multi-hit AoE carrying mixed damage types), or **equip every weapon at once** for a turn — all
packages active, a one-person army.
- **Cost:** high Strain. **Backlash:** the forge runs cold — can't `Conjure` or hold a `Wielding`
  mode for several rounds (you're unarmed while it cools).
- **Unlock:** story milestone + "won a fight by swapping to the right weapon at the right moment."

## Material rules
None carried — weapons manifest from Strain and **shatter** (last a few rounds, or break on a big
hit), so the smith re-forges constantly. **The trade is versatility-for-power:** every conjured tag
is a notch weaker than its specialist source. **Resolve** caps how much you can keep summoned at once.

## Combos
- **Sets up:** *whatever the fight needs* — `Entangled` to hold, `Fire` to ignite, `Knockback` to
  reposition, `Barrier` to guard, `Armor-Pierce` to crack a tank. The gap-filler.
- **Pays off (with this squad):** forge a **Chain** to `Entangle` what Gravity Anchor gathered;
  a **Flameblade** to torch Overgrowth's `Plant-Rich` zone *on your terms*; **Arm the Squad** to
  hand the gravity tank a riposte while it soaks. The toolbox that completes every other power's setup.
- **Cancelled by:** anti-magic / conjuration-suppression (`Sealed`-of-the-aether — a future
  counter), and per-weapon: whatever counters the tag you summoned (`Wet` blunts your Flameblade,
  etc.). A jack of all trades is *out-specialized* by any true specialist on their own axis.
- **Tension:** **choice paralysis is real** — the wrong weapon out is a wasted turn re-forging.
  And `Arm the Squad`'s temper tags can clash (hand a `Fire` blade to someone standing by your
  regenerator and you may `Cauterize` the heal).

## Environment
- **Loves:** anywhere — the armory doesn't care about terrain (its strength: no environmental denial)
- **Hates:** anti-magic / null zones; fights so fast there's no time to forge the right tool

## Strain profile
Flexible and choppy: cheap to swing, but **every swap spends tempo** — the skill is *reading the
fight a beat ahead* and forging the answer before you need it, not after. The opposite discipline
from a specialist who just does their one thing well.

## Tags minted
- **Mechanical:** `Conjure` *(manifest objects/weapons from will)*, `Arsenal` *(the multi-mode
  loadout system — a menu of `Wielding:` modes, extends the single Stance)*, `Wielding:X` *(weapon
  mode tags — `Wielding:Flameblade`/`Maul`/`Glaive`/`Chain`, each a borrowed tag package)*
- **Borrows (no new effects, by design):** `Fire`/`Burning`, `Crushing`/`Knockback`, `Piercing`,
  `Slashing`/`Entangled`, `Barrier`/`Cover`, `Armor-Pierce` — the jack-of-all-trades assembles the
  dictionary it didn't write.

*(Deferred to a later batch: **Quicksilver / Super Speed** — the action-economy striker, parked
alongside Blink and Probability Pull's eventual wiring.)*
