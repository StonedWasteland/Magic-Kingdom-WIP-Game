# Power 01 — Pyrokinesis

**Identity:** *Conjure and command fire from nothing. Never runs dry, but the world can take
your fire away.*

- **Domain:** Elemental
- **Specificity:** Broad (generative, versatile — lower raw ceiling than a narrow fire power)
- **Material:** None
- **Role:** Artillery / AoE
- **Intrinsic tags:** `Fire` `Generative` `Ranged` `Area-Capable` `Combo-Igniter`

## Expressions

### ◈ Offense — Firebolt *(starter)*
A lance of flame, Near or Far. Deals `Fire`, applies `Burning` (DoT) on a Control check.
- Reads `Oiled` / `Flammable` on target → bonus damage + **guaranteed** `Burning`.
- Reads `Wet` / `Doused` → damage halved.
- **Cost:** low Strain.

### ◈ Defense — Flame Veil
Wreathe yourself in distortion-heat. Ranged attackers take an accuracy penalty (heat haze);
anyone Engaged with you eats `Fire` retaliation. Applies `Hazard: Flame` to your position.
- **Cost:** medium Strain, **sustained** (drains per turn held — first real Strain decision).
- **Unlock (natural):** survive melee while fire is active several times.

### ◈ Utility — Cinderlight
Light, burn, melt, signal. In exploration nodes, auto-solves `Flammable Barrier`, `Frozen`,
`Webbed`, `Overgrown-Dry` obstacle tags.
- **Cost:** trivial Strain.
- **Unlock (insight):** near-free, early.

### ◈ Support — Forge-Heart
Channel *controlled* heat into an ally: cauterize (`Bleeding` → cleared), thaw
(`Frozen`/`Chilled` → cleared), or temper their weapon so their next strikes carry `Fire` +
`Burning`.
- **Cost:** medium Strain.
- **Unlock:** support-use milestone, or a Smith-Gifted mentor (insight).

### ◈ Signature — Conflagration *(capstone)*
Every `Burning`, `Oiled`, `On-Fire`, or `Flammable` tag in the zone chain-detonates. Huge area
`Fire`, and the zone gains a persistent `On-Fire` terrain hazard.
- The **payoff for setup play** — scales with how much burning you/your party built up first.
  A spammer gets a fizzle; a tactician gets an inferno.
- **Cost:** high Strain, real Backlash risk.
- **Unlock:** story milestone + the pattern "3+ enemies burning in one encounter."

## Material rules
None — never runs dry. **The trade:** the weakness isn't ammo, it's *denial.* `Water Source`
zones, `Rain`, and Fire-Resist enemies shut it down hard.

## Combos
- **Sets up:** `Burning`, `On-Fire` zones — fuel for its own Signature and for allies.
- **Pays off:** ally `Oiled`/oil-slick → ignite; a `Slowed`/`Rooted` enemy can't flee your
  `On-Fire` zone; plant Root-Bind + fire = trapped and cooking.
- **Cancelled by:** an allied Water-mage's `Wet`/`Doused` erases your `Burning` (party-comp
  tension — coordinate fire and water deliberately).

## Environment
- **Loves:** `Flammable` · `Oiled` · `Plant-Rich` (dry) · `Enclosed` (heat builds) · `Gas`
- **Hates:** `Water Source` · `Rain` · `Open Sky` (area dissipates) · `Metal Structures`

## Strain profile
Cheap to throw bolts all day; the interesting spend is sustaining Flame Veil or gambling on
Conflagration. Resolve gates how aggressive you can be.
