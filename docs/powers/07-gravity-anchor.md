# Power 07 — Gravity Anchor

**Identity:** *You don't ask for aggro — you take it. Bend gravity into a well around yourself and
the battlefield falls inward: enemies are dragged to you, pinned heavy, and crushed where they
stand. The line holds because there's nowhere else to go.*

- **Domain:** Conceptual (gravity)
- **Specificity:** Broad-but-indirect — warps space, never strikes directly
- **Material:** None — the well is sustained by upkeep, not carried
- **Role:** Tank / frontline-controller
- **Intrinsic tags:** `Conceptual` `Gravity` `Control` `Sustained` `No-Material`

> **The engine concept this power forces — ATTRACTION (forced movement *toward*).** Atlas gave us
> Knockback: shove a unit a band *away*. Gravity Anchor is its mirror — **Pull**: drag a unit a band
> *toward* a point. That single inversion makes a whole new kind of tank: where Metal Skin's
> `Taunting` is a tag enemies are *compelled to obey*, gravity doesn't ask — it **physically
> relocates** them. They can't hit your backline because they're no longer near it; the well has
> already yanked them to the front. Aggro as *physics*, not persuasion — and nothing resists it but
> `Anchored`. It also introduces a persistent **`Gravity Well`** zone (a band tag, like `On-Fire`,
> but it pulls and weighs instead of burns).

## Expressions

### ◈ Defense — Event Horizon *(starter — the well)*
Open a `Gravity Well` on your band and hold it. While sustained, at end of round every enemy is
**Pulled** one band toward you, and anything caught in the well is `Heavy` (can't flee, −Reflex)
and takes `Crushing`. The tank that gathers the whole fight onto itself.
- **Cost:** an action to open/close; **Strain per round** to sustain (upkeep, like a stance).

### ◈ Offense — Crush
Spike gravity on one target: heavy `Crushing` + pin them `Heavy`/`Rooted` (their own weight nails
them down). Best on the enemy the well already dragged close.
- **Cost:** low Strain.

### ◈ Utility — Deadweight
Make yourself the immovable point: `Anchored` (immune to all forced movement, including enemy
pulls/knockbacks), wrench open `Barred`/`Destructible Terrain`, or drag a distant `Object`/ally to
you. The fixed star the battle orbits.
- **Cost:** trivial while the well is up.

### ◈ Support — Lensing
Bend light-and-mass around an ally: warp incoming **ranged** attacks off-course (a gravity lens =
ranged `Cover` for a teammate), or **pull a Downed ally out of danger** to your band.
- **Cost:** medium Strain.
- **Unlock:** shelter allies from ranged fire a few times (natural).

### ◈ Signature — Singularity *(capstone)*
Collapse the well to a point: **every** enemy in the zone is dragged into your band at once,
crushed for massive `Crushing`, and left `Heavy` + `Rooted` — the whole enemy force stacked in one
square for the squad to delete.
- **Cost:** huge Strain dump. **Backlash:** the collapse leaves the well spent — no `Gravity Well`
  and no upkeep possible for several rounds (you're just a person again, fully exposed).
- **Unlock:** story milestone + "pulled 3+ enemies into one band in a fight."

## Material rules
None carried. The cost is **upkeep** — Strain each round the `Gravity Well` is open, capped by
Resolve — so a long hold bleeds you the way Metal Skin's stance does. **Resolve** governs how long
you can sustain the well; **Vitality** how much you can take while you're the lightning rod.

## Combos
- **Sets up:** `Heavy`, `Rooted`, and — the headline — **clustering.** Pulling the enemy force into
  one band is the perfect feed for any AoE or zone.
- **Pays off (huge with this very squad):** drag enemies **into a hazard band** — Glasstorm's shred,
  Overgrowth's roots, or a fire zone — and let the terrain do the killing. Gravity gathers; the
  zone-makers reap. Clustered, `Heavy` targets can't dodge Radiance's beams either.
- **Cancelled by:** `Anchored`/colossal enemies (immune to the Pull), **ranged** attackers (they
  hurt you from outside the well), `Flying`/untethered foes, and running your Strain dry.
- **Tension:** the well pulls **everything** — careless placement can drag an enemy *out* of a
  teammate's carefully-aimed trap, or yank a foe onto your own squishies if you mistime the collapse.

## Environment
- **Loves:** `Enclosed` · `Urban` (chokepoints to gather into) · any tight space where the pull has
  nowhere to leak
- **Hates:** `Open Sky` / open field (mass disperses) · `Featureless` (nothing to crush them
  against) · scattered, ranged-heavy fights

## Strain profile
The same **low-but-constant upkeep** rhythm as Metal Skin, but spent on *space* instead of *skin*.
The decision is *when to drop the well to recover* versus holding it one more round to keep the
enemy gathered — and whether to gamble the whole reserve on a Singularity that leaves you naked
after.

## Tags minted
- **Mechanical:** `Gravity`, `Pull` *(forced movement **toward** — the inverse of `Knockback`)*
- **Zone:** `Gravity Well` *(a band hazard that pulls + weighs each round)*
- **Status:** `Heavy` *(already owned by Metal Skin — Gravity Anchor leans on it hard)*, `Singularity`
- **Counter (already in dict):** `Anchored` is the hard resist to `Pull`
